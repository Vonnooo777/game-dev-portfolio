I created all of the gameplay and art, including the bouncing projectile mechanics, combat interactions, and reactive audio behavior. The challenge was making each collision and action feel clear and satisfying.

Technical highlight

Keeping combo feedback and upgrades in sync

I tied the combo display to the ball’s current color so the HUD reflects its gameplay state. I also refactored the code around the ball object to accommodate upgrades and unlocks that change how it behaves. The challenge was keeping that feedback consistent as the gameplay expanded.

The menu system was my largest overhaul. I iterated on the visual hierarchy, layout, and navigation flow to make information readable and each screen easier to understand. 03 / UNITY · C#

On Dreams And Nightmares

Playable demoPlay on itch.io ↗

A platformer built around movement, speed, and environmental interactions. I created all of the gameplay and art, from character controls and movement physics to the environments and visual effects.

Play the demo ↗Watch trailer ↗

Technical highlight

Learning Unity through movement and HUD systems

My first Unity game taught me C#, the object lifecycle, and the importance of cleaning up gameplay objects after use. I worked through physics integration and player speed limits, alongside displaying health and cooldowns clearly. Building the HUD was a particular challenge and helped me understand how much work goes into communicating gameplay state.
